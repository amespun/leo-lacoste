In the air

No engine, only warm air. You find a thermal, circle inside it to climb, and read the clouds to know where the next one is. You plan before you take off, and you stay calm when the plan changes.

  • Vol à voile
  • Learned in France
  • Solo pilot
  • Thermals and cross-country

What it gave me at work: patience, preparation, and calm decisions when conditions change.
