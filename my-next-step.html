My first 90 days.

How I would start, in any market. The method is the same one that worked at Safeguard Global.

  1. Days 1 to 30Listen and map

    Meet the key accounts and the team, learn the product and the buyers, map who signs and why.

  2. Days 31 to 60Build the pipeline

    Outbound into the accounts that fit, first meetings with decision makers, and early wins inside existing clients.

  3. Days 61 to 90Own the forecast

    A qualified pipeline I can defend, a plan per strategic account, and a written playbook the team can reuse.
