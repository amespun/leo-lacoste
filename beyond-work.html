Beyond work

What I build with AI, and what I do off the clock.

I use AI the way I sell: to spend less time on admin and more time with people. Here is what I use it for, and what keeps me busy when the laptop is closed.

Built with AI.

Two tools I run every week. One saves my sales team hours; the other helps me think about my own money.

Needs from Léo: check the details At work

A meeting assistant that writes my follow-ups

It listens to my customer calls (with their consent), writes the summary, pulls out the next steps and who owns them, and drafts the follow-up email. I read it, fix what needs fixing, and send. The CRM stays up to date without me typing it in.

  • Customer call
  • Transcript
  • Summary and next steps
  • Follow-up draft and CRM

Why it matters: more time selling, and no promise from a meeting gets forgotten.

An investing research desk, built with Claude

I built a small tool with Claude that reads company results and market news, explains them in plain words, and lays out the case for and against before I buy or sell. It does not trade for me; it makes me ask better questions.

  • Results and news
  • Plain-language summary
  • Case for and against
  • My decision

What it taught me: how to brief an AI like a colleague, and when not to trust it.

Off the clock.

The things I come back to, and what they have taught me about work.

In the air

I learned to fly gliders in France.

No engine: you climb on warm air and read the weather to stay up. It is the best training I know for patience, preparation and calm decisions when conditions change.

On the road

Travel is how I learn languages.

I have lived and worked in France, Germany, Spain (eight years in Barcelona) and now the Netherlands, and I travel to understand how people buy, eat and talk in each place. It is also why five languages feel normal to me.
