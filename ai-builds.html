What building them taught me

Brief an agent like a new colleague. The clearer the job, the context and the example, the better the work. The same is true for people.

Keep a human at the door. The agents prepare; I decide what a customer reads. Speed is worth nothing if trust breaks.

Start with the boring part. The best agent I have does the job I liked least.
