FAQ

A few questions, answered plainly.

Why have you worked at so many companies?

I chose breadth on purpose. Each move gave me a new market, a harder product or bigger accounts: from export sales, to software, to opening a country from zero. The result is that I ramp up fast, and I can sell what most reps can’t.

Which languages do you actually work in?

French and English are native. I sell in Spanish at a full professional level and work in German. My Dutch is elementary and improving, now that I live in the Netherlands.

What do you sell best?

Complex B2B deals with several stakeholders, where the buyer has to trust you before they trust the product. My best results came from HR tech and industrial software, sold to CFOs, HR directors and operations leaders.

Where are you based?

In the Utrecht area, the Netherlands, since 2025. Before that I lived and sold from Frankfurt, Strasbourg and, for eight years, Barcelona.

Have you managed people?

At Safeguard Global I managed and coached two BDRs and helped new hires get up to speed. At Amadeus I led implementation projects across internal teams and client stakeholders.

Do you understand the product side?

I completed CareerFoundry’s Product Management Program in 2025. It helps me bring customer feedback back to product teams in a form they can use.

Have you written anything?

Yes, a piece for Économie Matin on keeping employees in order to attract tomorrow’s talent.
